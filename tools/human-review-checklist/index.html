Free printable checklist

Keep human review visible.

Use this before AI-assisted work is published, sent, entered into a system or used to make a decision that affects someone else.

Check whether AI fits the task →

Human-reviewed does not mean glanced at

The reviewer needs enough context, authority and subject knowledge to recognize a plausible but wrong result.

Check each statement only when the reviewer can support it.

Stop condition

If the reviewer cannot check an item, do not hide the gap.

Correct the result, find a qualified reviewer, reduce the consequence, change the input or keep the work human. “The AI wrote it” does not transfer responsibility.

This is a practical communication and workflow checklist. It is not legal, compliance, clinical, security or professional advice.
