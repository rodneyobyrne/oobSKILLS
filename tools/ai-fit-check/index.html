Should AI help with this task?

Think about one real responsibility or workflow—not your whole job or business. This check helps you decide whether AI should assist with any part of it, where human review matters, and what to do next.

Private by design

Your assessment answers stay in this browser and are not saved. If you add a public URL, that URL is sent to the third-party Jina Reader service so the public page can be reviewed; your assessment answers are not sent. Do not enter private, login-protected, client, patient or employee information.

How are you considering using AI in this work?

Choose all that apply. Real work often involves more than one kind of AI support.

1. Does this task happen repeatedly?
2. Can you describe where the task begins and what a complete result looks like?
3. Do you have reliable source information the AI is permitted to use?
4. Would the task require private, regulated or sensitive personal information?
5. Could a wrong result materially affect someone's health, safety, rights, employment, eligibility, money or trust?
6. Is a capable person available to review the result before anyone relies on it?
7. Is one person clearly accountable for the final decision or published result?
8. Can you test this on a small scale and stop without causing harm or disrupting essential work?

    What this free tool cannot decide

    A promising task is not permission to use every tool.

    Vendor terms, security controls, legal obligations, organizational policy and the actual data involved still matter. Public-page research can add context, but it cannot establish permission to use private information. This is a practical starting check, not legal, security or compliance advice.
