Free decision tool

Should AI help with this task?

Choose one real task—not your whole job or business. This check helps you decide whether to test AI assistance, narrow the task or stop until important conditions are clearer.

Private by design

Your answers remain in this browser. This page does not send or save them. Do not enter private, client, patient or employee information.

1. Does this task happen repeatedly?
2. Can you describe where the task begins and what a complete result looks like?
3. Do you have reliable source information the AI is permitted to use?
4. Would the task require private, regulated or sensitive personal information?
5. Could a wrong result materially affect someone's health, safety, rights, employment, eligibility, money or trust?
6. Is a capable person available to review the result before anyone relies on it?
7. Is one person clearly accountable for the final decision or published result?
8. Can you test this on a small scale and stop without causing harm or disrupting essential work?

What this tool cannot decide

A promising task is not permission to use every tool.
