    What the tool is evaluating

    Volume matters less than the role those calls play.

    A construction company may receive fewer calls than a call center and still have a meaningful problem if those calls include project inquiries, active-job questions or scheduling requests that arrive while the team is working.

    • Missed or delayed calls create the exposure signal.
    • New-project calls raise the commercial consequence.
    • Jobsite, driving and after-hours gaps make consistent live answering harder.
    • Scheduling and bilingual demand increase the usefulness of a configured agent.
