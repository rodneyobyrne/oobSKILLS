Personalized working results

The next digital product is not another download.

A guided assessment should understand enough about your actual work to return a decision, a plan and a resource you can keep using after the tools change.

The product standard

Free resources provide the fundamentals. A paid assessment earns its price by applying those fundamentals to your situation and reducing the choices in front of you.

First build priority

AI Workday Review

For a small-business owner, operations lead or small team caught between “we should use AI” and “we do not want another tool or risk to manage.” The completed review will turn a longer survey into a personalized map of where AI may help, where it should not, and which single pilot is worth trying first.

The intended result

  • A keep-human, AI-assist, later and not-yet map of recurring work.
  • Three plausible opportunities and one prioritized 14-day pilot.
  • A clear split between AI support and human responsibility.
  • Permitted inputs, privacy cautions and a review standard.
  • Reusable working briefs for the selected use.
  • A portable result that does not depend on continued access to one platform.
  • A continue-alone, get-guidance or implementation next step.

Why it is not accepting payment this morning

The survey and output logic can be drafted quickly. Secure payment verification, private result generation, reliable delivery, privacy terms, failure recovery and testing cannot be responsibly treated as a static GitHub Pages download. The product will open only when those parts work together.

This is a service-shaped product. The buyer receives a tailored result. oobCREATIVE builds and maintains the system rather than manually producing every report.
