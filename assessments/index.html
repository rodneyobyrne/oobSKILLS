AI Workday Review

Find one responsible place to begin.

For a small-business owner, operations lead or small team caught between “we should use AI” and “we do not want another tool or risk to manage.” The review turns one recurring responsibility into a practical map of where AI may help, where it should not, and which single pilot is worth trying first.

Your completed WORKFILE includes

  • A pilot-now, prepare-first, smaller-support-task or keep-human decision.
  • Three plausible opportunities based on the recurring work you identify.
  • One bounded 14-day pilot with a human owner and stopping condition.
  • A clear split between AI support and human responsibility.
  • Permitted-input guidance, privacy cautions and a review standard.
  • Three reusable working briefs for preparation, review and handoff.
  • A portable Markdown result plus browser printing and PDF saving.
Your answers stay on your device. The review uses transparent browser-based rules rather than sending your responses to an AI model. A local draft helps protect your progress.

Idea-to-Test Review

Choose one direction without making it permanent.

For an experienced professional, owner or independent consultant who wants to compare promising possibilities without letting private refinement delay useful evidence. The review protects your credibility and room to change direction while turning one idea into a small market test.

Your completed WORKFILE includes

  • A ready-to-test, problem-first, narrow-the-test or credibility-first decision.
  • One test hypothesis connecting audience, problem, outcome and next step.
  • An honest evidence-and-assumption map with the riskiest assumption named.
  • The minimum credibility piece worth showing before the invitation.
  • A bounded field test with a decision date, success signal and stopping condition.
  • A clear list of what not to build before the test produces evidence.
  • Conversation, invitation and post-test decision briefs.
  • A portable Markdown result plus browser printing and PDF saving.
Choosing this test does not choose your identity. It gives one direction temporary contact with reality while other possibilities remain deliberately set aside.
