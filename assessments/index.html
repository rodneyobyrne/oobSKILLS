Customer Flow Health Review

Why is the business getting harder to run as it grows?

Customer information, follow-up and everyday work can become harder when the business grows faster than the way information moves between people and tools. That does not automatically mean the software is wrong.

The Customer Flow Health Review helps you see what appears to be working, where repeated handoffs are creating friction, where visibility is limited and what your next decision should be before you shop for technology.

Your result includes

  • One recognizable stage: Holding Up Well, Some Patchwork Is Showing, Successful but Stretched, People Are Bridging the Systems or Growth Is Amplifying the Gaps.
  • Five directional Customer Flow Health states: Communication, Information, Workflow, Capacity and Change Pressure.
  • A separate visibility note so “I don’t know” is not treated as evidence that something is failing.
  • What appears to be working as well as where friction is showing up.
  • A clear next decision and up to four places worth examining first.
  • Useful tool categories to consider only after the business problem is clearer.
  • A portable Markdown result plus browser printing and PDF saving.
No software pitch. Multiple tools are not automatically a problem. Keep what works, connect what should connect and replace only what has genuinely become a limitation.

Free · 5–7 minutes

AI Workday Review

Where can AI help without creating another problem?

Start with one recurring responsibility, not an AI product. The review helps you decide whether AI is useful now, whether preparation should come first or whether the work should remain human.

Your result includes

  • A pilot-now, prepare-first, smaller-support-task or keep-human decision.
  • Three plausible opportunities based on the recurring work you identify.
  • One bounded 14-day pilot with a human owner and stopping condition.
  • A clear split between AI support and human responsibility.
  • Permitted-input guidance, privacy cautions and a review standard.
  • Three reusable working briefs for preparation, review and handoff.
  • A portable Markdown result plus browser printing and PDF saving.

Free · About 10 minutes

Review one real responsibility.

Leave with a decision, a 14-day plan and three working briefs. No account, email or purchase is required.

Idea-to-Test Review

How can you test an idea without overbuilding it?

Turn one promising direction into a small field test before it becomes a permanent identity, offer or build. The review gives the idea contact with reality while preserving room to change direction.

Your result includes

  • A ready-to-test, problem-first, narrow-the-test or credibility-first decision.
  • One test hypothesis connecting audience, problem, outcome and next step.
  • An honest evidence-and-assumption map with the riskiest assumption named.
  • The minimum credibility piece worth showing before the invitation.
  • A bounded field test with a decision date, success signal and stopping condition.
  • A clear list of what not to build before the test produces evidence.
  • Conversation, invitation and post-test decision briefs.
  • A portable Markdown result plus browser printing and PDF saving.

Free · About 12 minutes

Move from private refinement to one honest test.

No account, email or purchase is required. Your answers and local draft stay in this browser.
