Live first release

AI Workday Review

For a small-business owner, operations lead or small team caught between “we should use AI” and “we do not want another tool or risk to manage.” The review turns a guided survey into one practical map of where AI may help, where it should not, and which single pilot is worth trying first.

Your completed WORKFILE includes

  • A pilot-now, prepare-first, smaller-support-task or keep-human decision.
  • Three plausible opportunities based on the recurring work you identify.
  • One bounded 14-day pilot with a human owner and stopping condition.
  • A clear split between AI support and human responsibility.
  • Permitted-input guidance, privacy cautions and a review standard.
  • Three reusable working briefs for preparation, review and handoff.
  • A portable Markdown result plus browser printing and PDF saving.

Why this release is free

Small teams should not have to buy a shallow score or short-lived prompt bundle to make a responsible first decision. This browser-based release provides the complete foundational result. Future paid versions must add real organizational value—shared participation, private server-side generation, policy alignment or implementation—not merely put this result behind a gate.

The tool does not send your answers to an AI model. Deterministic rules create the readiness decision on your device. A local browser draft protects you from losing progress.
