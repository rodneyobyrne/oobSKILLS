01 What actually organizes the customer relationship?

Start with the operating model, not a software category. A roofer, consultant, dental office and retailer can all need customer records for very different reasons.

Does the customer record include sensitive, regulated or specialized information?
02 Follow the customer in from the outside.

The first operational problem is often not the CRM. It is the moment a phone call, form, email or text becomes somebody’s responsibility.

Describe the real current sequence, including any workaround people rely on.

03 Decide which information is allowed to be “the truth.”

When two systems can both be edited as the master record, integration does not solve the conflict. It can simply distribute conflicting information faster.

04 Name the business problem worth fixing first.

The goal is not a cleaner technology diagram. It is a customer flow your team can operate without repeated copying, guessing or “did anyone call them?”

Would the team agree to one authoritative place for customer information if the workflow were clear?
