Useful answers

Start with the problem a buyer recognizes.

The short answer should be useful before we ask you to go anywhere else.

01

Do I need an AI receptionist or an answering service?

If customers call while you are working, start by separating the predictable handoff from the relationship. A good first system answers common questions, gathers approved information and makes the path to a person clear.

Compare the options →

02

What workflow should we automate first?

Look for repeated support work around a human decision: gathering context, organizing information, drafting, comparing, routing or preparing the next step.

03

Do we need an AI consultant, software, IT or a developer?

Often the first need is not a hire. It is enough structure to separate a workflow problem, a software problem, a security problem and something the team can handle internally.

04

Why is our website not turning attention into leads?

Most website problems begin before design. The visitor needs to recognize the offer, understand the value, compare the next step and trust that the path will not waste their time.

05

What information should employees never paste into AI?

Keep private information, high-consequence judgment, sensitive relationships, regulated data and unapproved source material inside a human-owned review process.
