Work in context

Measure the problem before you automate, redesign or rebuild.

Without a baseline and a completion signal, “better” becomes a feeling instead of a decision.

Before

Name the condition to improve.

Missed contacts, repeated steps, unclear approvals, confusing website paths, founder dependency or an incomplete digital project.

Intervention

Build the smallest useful change.

A workflow, integration, rewritten decision path, reusable knowledge resource, pilot or recovery phase.

After

Decide what changed and what did not.

Compare time, contacts, rework, decision clarity, review burden or another observable signal agreed to at the start.
