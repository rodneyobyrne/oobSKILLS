Common signal

People understand the value once you explain it personally, but the website does not carry the same clarity on its own.

What the work may establish

  • Primary audience and buying/decision context.
  • Clear service and problem language.
  • Positioning and value explanation without unsupported claims.
  • Homepage and service-page message hierarchy.
  • Calls to action matched to visitor readiness.
  • Proof requirements and gaps.
  • Searchable page headings and internal-link language.
  • Reusable core language for proposals, profiles and other channels.

Website clarity is not just shorter copy

The goal is to make the business easier to identify, compare, trust and act on. Complex work may still need nuance; the page needs a clear doorway into it.

Completion should improve self-selection

A qualified visitor should be able to recognize fit and the next step without requiring the owner or expert to personally translate the offer every time.
