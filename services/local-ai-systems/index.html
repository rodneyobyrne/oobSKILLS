The local businesses most likely to benefit are the ones where the owner or crew cannot stop mid-job to answer every call: home services, repair and maintenance, appointment-based businesses, hospitality support, professional services and other teams where responsiveness matters.

Start with a 14-day working pilot

  • Map one current customer-contact flow and the reasons it breaks down.
  • Decide what the system may answer, collect, schedule or route.
  • Write the approved source information and escalation rules.
  • Make automated assistance clear instead of impersonating a person.
  • Connect one existing calendar, phone number, form or follow-up channel where technically appropriate.
  • Test common calls, unusual requests, interruptions and failure states.
  • Review the results and decide whether to keep, revise or stop.

What remains human

Pricing exceptions, complaints, sensitive circumstances, professional advice, promises outside approved policy and any situation the system cannot understand should reach a named person or create a clear callback. The point is not to make a robot sound deceptively human. It is to make the business reliably reachable without hiding who is responsible.

Initial scope and price

A single-workflow local pilot starts at $950, plus the direct cost of any phone, scheduling, messaging or AI services selected. The price is confirmed before setup after a short written fit review. Larger integrations, custom software, regulated information and multi-location routing are scoped separately.

Not every problem needs AI. If a better voicemail, calendar rule, intake form or notification solves the issue more reliably, that is the recommendation.

Not an initial fit

  • Emergency dispatch or situations involving immediate health or safety risk.
  • Clinical, legal or financial advice.
  • Cold outbound AI calling or unsolicited text campaigns.
  • A system intended to conceal that the customer is interacting with automation.
  • Work that requires storing sensitive information before the privacy and security responsibilities are established.
