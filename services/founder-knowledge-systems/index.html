Knowledge + decision systems

Turn founder knowledge into systems your team can use.

Capture recurring standards, context and decision logic so capable people can prepare stronger work and make more routine decisions without everything returning to the founder.

Protect the standard

The objective is not to remove the founder from decisions that genuinely require their judgment. It is to stop using founder attention for context and corrections that can become reusable.

What the work may create

  • Map of recurring founder-dependent decisions and approvals.
  • Annotated examples showing quality standards in practice.
  • Decision rules and escalation criteria.
  • Approved source and context libraries.
  • Templates, checklists or first-draft structures.
  • AI-assisted preparation where it is useful and reviewable.
  • Clear authority boundaries for staff or collaborators.
  • Maintenance instructions so the system evolves with the business.

Start with actual corrections

The most useful knowledge is often visible in what the founder repeatedly changes, rejects, rewrites or explains. Those moments reveal the judgment the system needs to carry.

Completion should reduce avoidable returns

The test is whether team members can produce stronger first passes, make clearer routine decisions and know which exceptions genuinely need founder attention.

First step

Identify one recurring return loop.
