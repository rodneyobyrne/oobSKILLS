How it works

You provide the knowledge. The agent handles the repeatable conversation.

We start with the calls your business already receives, not a list of AI features. The useful first version should solve one clear customer-contact problem without creating another system for you to babysit.

1

2

3

You receive the useful information back so the right person can follow up, step in or continue the relationship.

Human responsibility stays visible

You decide what the agent knows—and what should stay human.

We configure the agent around approved business information and clear boundaries. A useful voice agent should know when to answer, when to collect information and when the next step belongs to a person.

Your answers.The agent works from information you choose to provide.
Your boundaries.Exceptions, sensitive situations and anything outside the defined job can be handed back to a human.
Your relationship.The goal is better coverage and a cleaner handoff—not removing people from the customer experience.
