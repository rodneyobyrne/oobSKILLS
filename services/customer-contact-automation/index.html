Automation, intake and follow-up systems for customer-facing businesses.

Start with one lost connection

The right solution may be a simple rule, a form, CRM automation, scheduling integration, AI receptionist or a custom combination.

What the work may connect

  • Inbound calls and after-hours coverage.
  • Website forms and structured intake.
  • Scheduling and rescheduling.
  • CRM or practice-management records.
  • Routing and callback ownership.
  • Estimate, proposal or appointment follow-up.
  • Approved FAQs and customer information.
  • Escalation to a person for sensitive or exceptional situations.

Human handoff is part of the system

Automation should not trap customers or make staff reconstruct what happened. Each workflow needs approved scope, a record of the interaction, escalation rules and a named owner for exceptions.

Completion should reduce lost contact

Measure whether the workflow captures more usable customer contact, shortens response delay or reduces repeated follow-up work without creating confusion or review burden.
