# oobSKILLS Content and Product Strategy

**Selected direction:** August 23, 2026
**Applies to:** skills.oobcreative.com
**Status:** Free WORKFILE suite and local-service checkout implemented; organization-grade paid assessment fulfillment remains a later infrastructure decision

## Site role

skills.oobcreative.com is the practical, self-service side of oobCREATIVE. It should not become a generic AI education site or a storefront of disconnected downloads.

The site helps capable people:

1. recognize where work or communication is stuck;
2. understand what they are trying to protect;
3. use genuinely useful free help;
4. buy a bounded assessment when a personalized working result has value;
5. move into human support only when judgment, authority, alignment or implementation is involved.

## Commercial rule

> Free resources help someone recognize, understand and begin. Paid products apply the structure to their situation and leave them with a usable result. Human services provide judgment, alignment and implementation.

Do not compete with low-price prompt bundles on quantity. Give foundational prompts, checklists and recognition tools away. Charge for a bounded decision, portable working resource, organization-use rights or human implementation.

### First personalized assessment

**AI Workday Review - free browser release is live**

Search-facing promise: Find the first useful AI workflow for a small business or small team.

Current output:

- readiness decision and work-area map;
- three prioritized opportunities;
- one bounded 14-day pilot;
- explicit human/AI responsibilities;
- review standard;
- three reusable working briefs;
- portable Markdown context file; and
- decisions about what not to automate.

The complete foundational version is free. It processes answers with deterministic browser-side rules, saves a draft only in local browser storage and produces a printable and downloadable Markdown WORKFILE. It does not transmit answers to an AI model or require an account.

A future paid organization edition must add real value rather than place the existing result behind a gate. Candidate value includes multi-participant input, private server-side processing, shared approval, organization-specific policy alignment, durable account history or implementation support.

Do not publish a paid assessment price or checkout until secure payment verification, private generation, automated delivery, license, refund, privacy, support and failure-recovery terms exist. Static GitHub Pages cannot securely provide those functions by itself.

Required fulfillment path:

1. Stripe Checkout or a Payment Link collects payment.
2. A secure server endpoint verifies the Stripe event and completed Checkout Session.
3. The buyer receives time-limited access to the assessment, not a public file URL.
4. The survey sends only the approved response data to the private assessment service.
5. Deterministic rules establish the core recommendation before AI writes or adapts the explanation.
6. The system generates a readable result and portable Markdown or PDF copy.
7. Errors create a recoverable delivery state instead of silently losing the purchase.

Stripe secret keys, webhook secrets and model credentials must never be placed in the public GitHub Pages repository. Stripe’s fulfillment guidance requires server-side handling of completed payments; a browser redirect alone is not reliable fulfillment.

### Live local offer

**Local AI Phone, Scheduling and Intake Systems**

Audience: owner-led and small-team businesses in Aspen, Basalt, Carbondale, Glenwood Springs and nearby communities where the owner or crew cannot consistently interrupt the work to answer every customer contact.

Entry situations:

- missed inbound or after-hours calls;
- repeated scheduling and rescheduling;
- customer intake that arrives incomplete;
- requested estimates, callbacks and reminders that lose momentum.

Offer: one $950, 14-day working pilot plus direct software and usage costs. The pilot maps one workflow, defines approved information and escalation, builds the smallest useful system, tests normal and failure cases, and ends with a keep, revise or stop decision.

Purchase path: the service page establishes scope and exclusions, then requests a short written fit review. After fit, intended start date and cancellation terms are confirmed, the customer pays through the live Stripe Payment Link. The payment CTA is intentionally contained on the detailed service page rather than distributed across awareness pages.

Position around the business result, not the phrase “local AI consultant.” Current local search results show little evidence of an established Roaring Fork Valley search category for that title. Locality is a trust, context and implementation advantage; the demand language is missed calls, appointment scheduling, intake and follow-up.

Human consultation remains visible but bounded. Email is the first qualification step. A focused local coffee, phone call or written response is selected only when it helps the decision. The business does not depend on workshops, constant networking or Rodney becoming a public social personality.

### Authority content requiring a later decision

**What Changed in AI This Week — and What Matters to a Small Team**

A short weekly webcast could translate current AI news into three distinctions: what changed, who should care, and whether any action is warranted. It fits the brand when it reduces noise instead of repeating it. It should not launch until Rodney chooses a sustainable cadence and format. Test a four-episode season or twice-monthly briefing before making a permanent weekly promise.

Recommended editorial structure:

1. one material change;
2. one overhyped story that does not require action;
3. one practical small-business or nonprofit implication;
4. one responsibility or human boundary;
5. one question to carry into the week.

## SEO and metadata rules

- One primary search intent per indexable page.
- Lead with descriptive search language; use “assessment” or the specific outcome before inventing a branded format label.
- Every page needs a unique title, description, H1, canonical URL and social title/description.
- Keep core useful content in crawlable HTML.
- Use `Product` and `Offer` structured data only when a real product, accurate price, availability and purchase path are live.
- Use `Service` for service pages and `WebApplication` only for functioning web tools.
- No duplicate pillar pages across skills.oobcreative.com and oobcreative.com.
- The main site owns broad agency authority. The skills site owns self-service tools, product explanations and practical-AI education.
- Do not pursue bulk AI content, artificial GEO tactics, invented mentions or unverified claims.

## Page writing sequence

Every pathway, tool, product and service page should move through:

1. recognition;
2. protected value;
3. useful distinction;
4. desired movement;
5. concrete output;
6. boundary; and
7. a small number of next choices.

Do not move from insecurity to pressure to exaggerated promise.

## Claim rules

- Do not convert intended outcomes into past results.
- Do not invent testimonials, metrics, client permission or evidence.
- Use samples and capability descriptions until a verified proof register exists.
- Keep privacy, dignity, consent and human accountability visible.

## Measurement

Track meaningful movement rather than page views alone:

- tool start and completion;
- result use, copy or print;
- product preview and checkout start;
- purchase and completion;
- refund and support rate;
- qualified service progression; and
- whether users report fewer choices and a clearer next action.

## Maintenance boundary

Automated delivery does not make an AI product permanently maintenance-free. Version the products, explain assumptions, limit support and review product logic, platform dependencies, privacy and language at least quarterly.
