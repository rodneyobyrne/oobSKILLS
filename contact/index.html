For project or service questions, send the useful context.

A short description is enough to begin. Include what you are trying to improve, where the work is breaking down, who is affected and which systems are already involved. If there is a deadline or a current project that needs recovery, include that too.

You do not need to prepare a formal brief before reaching out. The first job is to understand the problem well enough to choose a sensible next step.

Keep sensitive information out of ordinary email.

Do not send passwords, account credentials, payment-card information, patient records, client files, employee records or other confidential or regulated information by ordinary email. Describe the type of information or workflow instead. If protected information becomes necessary for an approved project, the handling method should be agreed before it is shared.

Privacy and accessibility questions can come to the same place.

Email hello@oobcreative.com and identify whether the question is about privacy, accessibility or another site issue. You can also review the current Privacy Policy and Accessibility commitment before reaching out. For an accessibility barrier, include the page and what you were trying to do so the problem can be reproduced and addressed.
