Privacy

Use the tools without handing us more information than the work requires.

This page describes the current skills.oobcreative.com tools as of August 23, 2026. It will be updated before payment, accounts, saved results or server-based analysis are added.

A practical rule

Do not enter confidential, regulated, client, patient, employee or personally identifying information into a free tool.

The current Audience Review, AI Fit Check and Human Review Checklist operate in your browser and do not require an account or email address.

Information you enter into the current tools

The AI Fit Check and Human Review Checklist do not transmit or save your answers through oobCREATIVE application code. The current Audience Review generates its result in your browser. It does not send its answers to an external analysis endpoint in the published site configuration.

Do not use these tools for confidential or regulated information. Describe a type of work or situation rather than naming private individuals or pasting protected records.

Website hosting and technical logs

The site is hosted through GitHub Pages. Like most hosting services, the provider may process technical request information such as an IP address, browser information, requested page and time for security, reliability and service operation. Their handling of that information is governed by their own policies.

Email and service inquiries

If you contact oobCREATIVE by email, the message and contact information you provide are used to respond and manage the resulting business conversation. Email is not an appropriate channel for confidential health, legal, employment or similarly sensitive records. A local AI-system inquiry should describe the workflow without including customer records or other protected information.

Payments and paid assessments

No paid assessment checkout is currently live on this site. Before a paid product opens, this policy and the purchase page will identify the payment processor, information required for the transaction, fulfillment process, license, refund terms and support boundary.

Cookies and analytics

The current repository does not include oobCREATIVE advertising cookies or a behavioral analytics integration. Hosting infrastructure may use essential technical mechanisms required to deliver and protect the site.

Changes

This policy will be updated when the site begins collecting additional information, saving results, using server-based analysis, adding analytics, opening accounts or accepting payment.
