Privacy

Use the tools without handing us more information than the work requires.

The interactive reviews are designed to give you a useful result in your browser without requiring an account or email address.

The short answer

Your assessment answers are not automatically sent to oobCREATIVE. If a tool offers a human-review option, you choose whether to share a result.

Are assessment answers sent to oobCREATIVE?

No, not by the assessment itself. The Audience Review, AI Fit Check, Human Review Checklist, AI Workday Review, Idea-to-Test Review and Customer Flow Health Review operate in your browser and do not require an account or email address.

The AI Fit Check does not transmit or save your answers through oobCREATIVE application code. The Audience Review, Human Review Checklist, AI Workday Review, Idea-to-Test Review and Customer Flow Health Review generate their results in your browser and do not send answers to an external analysis endpoint or AI model.

What is saved in the browser?

The Audience Review, Human Review Checklist, AI Workday Review, Idea-to-Test Review and Customer Flow Health Review use browser storage for interruption recovery so you do not necessarily lose progress if a page reloads. These drafts are stored on the device and browser you are using; they are not oobCREATIVE account records.

You can remove a draft with the tool’s “Clear saved draft” or “Clear saved answers” control, or by clearing this site’s browser data.

Do not enter confidential, regulated, client, patient, employee or other sensitive personally identifying information into a free tool. Describe the type of work or situation rather than pasting protected records.

What happens if I share a Customer Flow result for human review?

Sharing is a separate action you choose after the result is created. The Customer Flow Health Review prepares a short summary and opens your email application only after you select “Share My Results for Review.” You can see, edit or delete the prepared text before deciding whether to send the email.

Until you send that email, the assessment has not transmitted the result to oobCREATIVE. If you do send it, the message and contact information you provide are handled like any other email inquiry and used to respond to the request.

Does the website collect any technical information?

The site is hosted through GitHub Pages. Like most hosting services, the hosting provider may process technical request information such as an IP address, browser information, requested page and time for security, reliability and service operation. Their handling of that information is governed by their own policies.

This distinction is why oobCREATIVE describes the assessments as browser-based and not automatically sending your answers rather than claiming that no technical data of any kind can ever be processed by the hosting infrastructure.

What happens when I email oobCREATIVE?

If you contact oobCREATIVE by email, the message and contact information you provide are used to respond and manage the resulting business conversation. Email is not an appropriate channel for confidential health, legal, employment or similarly sensitive records. A service inquiry should describe the workflow without including customer records or other protected information.

How are service payments handled?

The Local AI Systems 14-Day Pilot currently uses a Stripe-hosted Payment Link after written fit and start-date confirmation. Stripe collects and processes the transaction, business name, contact and payment information under Stripe’s own terms and privacy practices. This website does not receive or store full card numbers. oobCREATIVE receives the customer and transaction information needed to confirm payment and provide the purchased service.

Does this site use advertising cookies or behavioral analytics?

This site does not use oobCREATIVE advertising cookies or behavioral analytics. Hosting infrastructure may use essential technical mechanisms required to deliver and protect the site. Browser storage used for assessment drafts is functional storage, not advertising tracking.

What if the data handling changes?

This policy will be updated when the site’s data handling changes.

How can I ask a privacy question?

Email hello@oobcreative.com with a question about this site or its current tools.
