Privacy

Use the tools without handing us more information than the work requires.

This page describes the current browser-based tools, local draft storage, email inquiries and Stripe service payment pathway as of August 23, 2026.

A practical rule

Do not enter confidential, regulated, client, patient, employee or personally identifying information into a free tool.

The Audience Review, AI Fit Check, Human Review Checklist and AI Workday Review operate in your browser and do not require an account or email address.

Information you enter into the tools

The AI Fit Check and Human Review Checklist do not transmit or save your answers through oobCREATIVE application code. The Audience Review and AI Workday Review generate their results in your browser and do not send answers to an external analysis endpoint or AI model.

The AI Workday Review saves an interruption-recovery draft in local browser storage on the device you use. The draft is not an oobCREATIVE account record. You can remove it with the “Clear saved draft” control or by clearing this site’s browser data.

Do not use these tools for confidential or regulated information. Describe a type of work or situation rather than naming private individuals or pasting protected records.

Website hosting and technical logs

The site is hosted through GitHub Pages. Like most hosting services, the provider may process technical request information such as an IP address, browser information, requested page and time for security, reliability and service operation. Their handling of that information is governed by their own policies.

Email and service inquiries

If you contact oobCREATIVE by email, the message and contact information you provide are used to respond and manage the resulting business conversation. Email is not an appropriate channel for confidential health, legal, employment or similarly sensitive records. A local AI-system inquiry should describe the workflow without including customer records or other protected information.

Stripe service payments

The Local AI Systems 14-Day Pilot uses a Stripe-hosted Payment Link after written fit and start-date confirmation. Stripe collects and processes the transaction, business name, contact and payment information under Stripe’s own terms and privacy practices. This website does not receive or store full card numbers. oobCREATIVE receives the customer and transaction information needed to confirm payment and provide the purchased service.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not use oobCREATIVE advertising cookies or behavioral analytics. Hosting infrastructure may use essential technical mechanisms required to deliver and protect the site. Browser local storage used for the AI Workday Review draft is functional storage, not advertising tracking.

Changes

This policy will be updated when the site’s data handling changes.
