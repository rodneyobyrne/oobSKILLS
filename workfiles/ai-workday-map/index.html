Find the first useful AI workflow for your business.

Not 100 possible uses. One bounded pilot you can understand, review and stop if it does not improve the work.

Why it is not on sale yet

The product will open for purchase only after the output has been tested against real small-business scenarios and the automated delivery is complete.

The AI Workday Map is being designed for a small-business owner, operations lead or small team that has access to AI but needs a defensible place to begin.

The situation it is meant to change

You can see possibilities, but demonstrations and tool lists have not answered the practical questions: What work is suitable? What remains human? What information is permitted? Who reviews the result? How will you know the change helped?

What the finished WORKFILE is intended to produce

  • A map of tasks that should remain human, may benefit from AI assistance, could be explored later or should not use AI yet.
  • Three prioritized opportunities rather than an overwhelming idea list.
  • One selected 14-day pilot with a defined beginning, result and stopping condition.
  • A visible split between AI support and human responsibility.
  • A human-review standard tied to the actual consequence.
  • Three reusable working briefs for the selected use case.
  • A readable result and portable Markdown context file.
  • Clear decisions about what not to automate.

What it will ask about

  • Recurring work that consumes attention or causes delay.
  • The decisions and relationships that still require human judgment.
  • Available source information and data sensitivity.
  • Existing tools, attempts and failure points.
  • Review capacity, ownership and acceptable consequence.
  • What successful improvement would look like.

What it will not do

It will not provide legal or compliance advice, approve a vendor, evaluate enterprise security, establish organizational policy, authorize use of sensitive data or repair unclear leadership decisions. Those conditions require qualified people and organizational participation.

The free AI Fit Check may be all you need. Use it first. The paid WORKFILE becomes useful when you want a reusable plan and working briefs built around your situation.
