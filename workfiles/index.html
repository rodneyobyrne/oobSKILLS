A WORKFILE is not valuable because it contains more pages, prompts or templates. It is valuable when it helps someone reach a better decision and carry that decision into the work.

Every finished WORKFILE should include:

  • A recognizable starting situation.
  • The value or responsibility that must not be lost.
  • A small number of choices rather than endless options.
  • A practical decision or test.
  • Clear human ownership and review.
  • A readable result and a portable Markdown version where useful.
  • Boundaries explaining what the product cannot solve.
  • A next step that can include continuing independently.

When a WORKFILE is not enough

A self-guided product should not pretend it can establish decision authority, align stakeholders, govern sensitive information, capture complex tacit judgment or implement a workflow across a team. Those are human and organizational responsibilities.

Use the free layer first

You may not need to buy anything.

Use a free tool to understand the situation. Move to a WORKFILE only when a personalized, reusable result would materially help.
