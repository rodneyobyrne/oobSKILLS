oobCREATIVE · AI Voice Agent

Service terms + statement of work

Clear responsibilities before the agent goes live.

These terms describe the standard Base and Connected Voice Agent service. A Partner engagement receives a separate written scope.

1. Service

oobCREATIVE will configure, test and support the AI Voice Agent services included in the purchased plan. The purchased plan and checkout record identify the setup fee, recurring monthly fee and included usage.

2. Setup

Setup includes a working session, approved business knowledge, voice and behavior configuration, included connections, test calls, reasonable initial tuning and launch preparation. Setup timing begins after oobCREATIVE receives the information, permissions and access reasonably needed to perform the work.

3. Client responsibilities

The client will provide accurate business information, policies, prices, instructions, escalation contacts, scheduling rules and authorized system access. The client is responsible for reviewing and approving the agent before live use and for notifying oobCREATIVE when business information materially changes.

4. AI limitations and human judgment

AI systems can misunderstand callers, produce incorrect responses or behave unpredictably. The agent is not a substitute for professional judgment, emergency response or regulated advice. The client and oobCREATIVE will define appropriate human handoffs. The client remains responsible for decisions, policies and business commitments made outside the agent's approved scope.

5. Launch approval

The agent will not be intentionally placed into live customer use until the agreed test and approval step is complete. Approval confirms that the client has reviewed the configured scope and authorizes launch.

6. Usage and third-party costs

Base includes 250 minutes per month and Connected includes 500 minutes per month. Additional usage is $0.25 per minute. Paid third-party apps, unusual carrier charges, custom integrations and material scope changes are separate when required and are discussed before they are added.

7. Changes and support

Routine answer updates and small tuning changes are included. New workflows, additional integrations, major restructuring or substantial new capabilities may require a separate quote or plan change.

8. Billing and cancellation

The setup fee is a one-time charge. Monthly service is recurring and continues until canceled. Cancellation stops future recurring service according to the effective cancellation date; amounts already earned or incurred remain due. Any required transition of call routing, connected systems or client-controlled accounts will be coordinated as part of offboarding.

9. Recording, privacy and regulated uses

The client is responsible for identifying legal, privacy, consent or industry-specific requirements that apply to its use of the agent. Call recording and transcription are configured only where appropriate. Regulated, sensitive or unusual uses may require a separate scope and safeguards.

10. Human support

Rodney or another human on his team will be the human point of contact for onboarding and service coordination. The AI line may operate 24/7; oobCREATIVE human support is not represented as staffed 24/7.

Last updated September 10, 2026. These standard terms are part of the Voice Agent purchase path and may be supplemented by a written plan-specific scope when needed.
