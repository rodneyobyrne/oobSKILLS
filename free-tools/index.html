Choose the right level

Use a self-guided tool when the decision can stay small.

These tools are useful when you can name one question, work without confidential information and keep a capable person responsible for the result.

Bring in human support when:

  • Several people need to agree on ownership or standards.
  • Sensitive information, policy, legal obligations or high-consequence decisions are involved.
  • The real obstacle is an unclear workflow, approval or leadership decision.
  • You need a system tested and implemented rather than another recommendation.
