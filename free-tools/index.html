What free means here

Free help addresses uncertainty and makes a real beginning possible.

It does not mean withholding the useful part until the final screen. It also does not mean pretending a checklist can solve an organizational problem.

Use a free tool when:

  • You need to recognize the kind of problem before choosing a service.
  • You want a safe first action without committing to a project.
  • You need language for an internal conversation.
  • You want to test the oobCREATIVE approach before purchasing anything.

Move beyond a free tool when:

  • Several people need to agree on ownership or standards.
  • Sensitive information, policy, legal obligations or high-consequence decisions are involved.
  • Your organization needs durable shared history or private processing.
  • You need implementation rather than another recommendation.

Next level

A paid offer must add more than personalization.

It earns its price through shared decisions, policy alignment, secure organization features, human judgment or implementation—not by hiding the useful foundation.
